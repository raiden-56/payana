Straight answers

Before you sign up.

Does {{ brand.name }} take a cut of my fare?

No. The customer pays you the fare you quoted, directly. If a booking fee is ever switched on, it is charged to the customer on top and shown to them before they accept.

Do I need to install an app?

No — it works in your phone’s browser and can be added to your home screen. One thing to know: a web app can share your location only while it is open on screen. Keep the dashboard open when you are available so nearby riders can see how close you are; if it has been closed for a while, we show you as “last seen” rather than pretending to know where you are.

How long does verification take?

Documents are reviewed in the order they arrive, and you are notified as soon as there is a decision. If something is unclear, we tell you exactly which document and why, and you upload a new photo.

What if I have to cancel a booking?

You can cancel from the booking, with a reason. Cancellations count: your cancellation rate is shown on your profile, so cancel only when you must.
