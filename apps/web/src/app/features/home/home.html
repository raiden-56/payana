More in the help centre
Is the quote the final price?

Yes. A driver's quote is for the trip exactly as you described it, and it lists what is included — fuel, tolls, parking, waiting time. If you change the plan on the way, agree the difference with the driver before you set off again.

How do I pay?

You pay the driver directly, in cash or by UPI, when the trip is done. {{ brand.name }} does not hold your money for the ride.

What does “verified” mean?

Before a driver is listed, our team reviews their identity, driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance, permit and fitness certificate. Documents are tracked to their expiry date; a driver whose papers lapse stops appearing in search until they are renewed.

Can I book for tomorrow, or for an airport run?

Yes. Choose “Later” for a local trip at a set time, or “Outstation” for stations, airports and other towns. Drivers quote in advance and the booking is confirmed when you accept one.

Who sees my phone number?

Nobody, until you accept a quote. After that, only the driver of that booking can call you — and only while the booking is active.
